The catalogue lists the resources in your collection, grouped by type.

<Image src={catalogueImage} alt="HERITRACE catalogue" width={800} height={600} loading="eager" />

The catalogue has two parts:

1.  **Categories** (left): the types of resource in your collection, such as "Journal Article" or "Book", each with the number of resources it holds. Click a category to see its resources.

2.  **Resources** (right): the resources of the selected category. Here you can:
    - change their order with the **Sort by** menu;
    - scroll down to see more, as the next resources load when you reach the end of the list;
    - click a title to open the page of that resource.

:::note
HERITRACE is designed for curating a collection, and the catalogue offers no search yet.
:::
