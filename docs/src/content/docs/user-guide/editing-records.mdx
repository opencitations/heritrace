Click a resource in the catalogue to open its page, which shows all its information.

<Image src={resourceImage} alt="Page of a resource in HERITRACE" width={800} height={600} />

### Changing a resource

The buttons below the title let you edit the resource, delete it and open its history. In edit mode you can:

-   change a value by clicking it;
-   add information with **Add property**;
-   link the resource to other resources, or change the existing links.

HERITRACE checks each change as you make it. Click **Save changes** to keep your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them.

When a field links to another resource, such as an author or an identifier, that resource opens in a window of its own, where you can change it.

### Deleting a resource and viewing its history

- **Delete resource** moves the resource to the [recycle bin](../recycle-bin), from which you can restore it.
- **Time machine** shows every version of the resource.

### Resources that link to this one

Below the information of the resource, the **Resources referencing this** section lists the other resources that link to the one you are viewing.

<Image src={resourcesReferencingThisImage} alt="Section listing the resources that link to the current one" width={800} height={600} />

For example, the page of a journal issue lists the articles published in it. Click one of them to open its page.

### Similar resources

At the bottom of the page, the **Similar resources** section lists resources that may be duplicates of the one you are viewing.

<Image src={similarResourcesImage} alt="Section listing similar resources, each with a Merge button" width={800} height={600} />

If one of them describes the same thing, click **Merge** to combine the two. The guide on [merging resources](../merging-records) describes each step.
