When two resources describe the same thing, you can merge them into one.

### How HERITRACE finds duplicates

HERITRACE looks for resources of the same type that share some details, such as the title, an identifier or the name of a person. It lists them as possible duplicates; you decide whether they really are.

### Step 1: start the merge

Open the page of the resource you want to keep.

<Image src={similarResourcesImage} alt="The Similar resources section of a resource page, with a Merge button next to each resource" width={800} height={600} />

1.  Scroll down to the **Similar resources** section at the bottom of the page.
2.  Find the resource you want to merge into the one you are viewing.
3.  Click **Merge** next to it.

### Step 2: check what will happen

A confirmation page summarises the merge.

<Image src={confirmMergeImage} alt="HERITRACE page for confirming the merge of two resources" width={800} height={600} />

It shows two boxes:
-   **Merge and delete**: the resource you chose from the Similar resources list, which will be deleted.
-   **Keep**: the resource whose page you started from, which will remain and receive the information of the other.

Below the boxes, the page explains what the merge does:

-   all the information of the resource to delete is added to the one you keep;
-   links from other resources to the deleted one now point to the one you keep;
-   only the resource you keep remains.

### Step 3: confirm

Click **Confirm merge**.

:::tip[Can I undo a merge?]
Yes. Open the [time machine](../tracking-changes) of the resource you kept and restore the version before the merge: HERITRACE also brings back the deleted resource and its links.
:::
